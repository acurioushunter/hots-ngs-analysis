# CCS draft plan: the damage squeeze, pick order, toss and maps

Written 2026-10-26. Every number comes from `python -I analyze_ccs_draft_habits.py` over all 23 CCS games this season (16-7, games through Oct 14), the 6 between us, our 25 games, CCS's older seasons from the replay export (S18 and S20), Hunter's Storm League games since Oct 26 2025 (on disk to Oct 12) and the HealsOnly team games. Nothing played after Oct 14 is on disk. Six games between us is a small sample, so read every line as a lean. Where a reason comes from the hero kits and not the numbers, it says so.

This replaces sections 1, 3, 4, 5 and 6 of `CCS_DRAFT_CHEAT_SHEET.md`. Its map playbook (section 7) still stands. Dates are match nights in Central time (the match pages store UTC, which is why older docs say Aug 19 and Sep 23).

## 1. What CCS actually does to us: the damage squeeze

**They spend their bans on Hunter and chelsi, and only on them.**
| Game | CCS round one bans | CCS round two ban | chelsi ended on | Hunter ended on | Result |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Sky Temple, Aug 18 | Stukov, Dehaka | Thrall | Tychus (slot 14) | Junkrat (5) | W |
| Dragon Shire, Aug 18 | Junkrat, Thrall | Tychus | Sylvanas (5) | Chromie (14), 6 deaths | L |
| Alterac Pass, Aug 18 | Junkrat, The Lost Vikings | Thrall | Ragnaros (16) | Sylvanas (6) | L |
| Volskaya, Sep 22 | Junkrat, Chromie | Tychus | Thrall (15) | Sylvanas (8) | W |
| Garden, Sep 22 | Junkrat, Chromie | Thrall | Tychus (16) | Sylvanas (7) | L |
| Infernal Shrines, Sep 22 | Junkrat, Chromie | Thrall | Tassadar (15) | Falstad (14), 4 deaths | L |

- **Since the first game, 14 of their 15 hero bans hit a hero Hunter or chelsi plays.** Junkrat first in all five, Chromie in the last three, then Thrall or Tychus with the round two ban in all six games. Since that first game they have not banned our healer's, tank's or offlaner's heroes once.
- **Their round two ban always takes whichever of Thrall and Tychus is left.** In five of six games both were still open when they banned, because we had not picked for chelsi yet.
- **We have never locked chelsi's hero before that ban.** chelsi's hero came in our last picks (slots 14 to 16) in five games, and on Dragon Shire chelsi took Sylvanas at slot 5 while Tychus was still open. Tychus was then banned and Hunter ended on Chromie at slot 14.
- **When chelsi got Tychus or Thrall we went 2-1 against them. When chelsi did not, 0-3.** Over the whole season: both of you on your best heroes (Hunter on Sylvanas or Junkrat, chelsi on Tychus or Thrall) 9-2, one of you 6-3, neither 1-4.
- Against other teams it did not matter that chelsi picked late (12-4 when chelsi was picked in our last picks), because they do not ban chelsi's heroes. CCS does.

**Why this is fixable.** They have three bans. Between you there are five heroes worth banning: Junkrat, Chromie and Sylvanas for Hunter, Tychus and Thrall for chelsi. Two of their bans come before any pick and one comes after our first pick turn. If both of you are locked in our first pick turn, their third ban has nothing of ours left to hit. Their whole plan against us relies on us picking damage last, and we did that every game.

**So the order flips: damage first, support last.** Our healer, tank and offlaner heroes have not been banned by CCS since that first game. They can still be picked by CCS (WitsEnd and ûltear play Rehgar, Valkamer plays Johanna and Muradin), so each has a fallback below.

The shared heroes are Sylvanas, Tychus and Chromie. Hunter's own are Junkrat, Orphea and Cassia. chelsi's own are Thrall, Tassadar and Gul'dan. Valla is 4-6 for Hunter over the last year, so it is out of the plan.

**The rules for the two of you:**
1. **Hunter keeps Sylvanas, chelsi keeps Tychus or Thrall.** On Dragon Shire chelsi took Sylvanas while Tychus was open, Tychus was banned next, and Hunter ended on Chromie at slot 14 (6 deaths). We lost that one.
2. **Pick the contested hero first.** Sylvanas and Tychus are contested (CCS plays both). Thrall, Orphea and Cassia are not (CCS never plays them), so they can wait if they have to.
3. **Between Tychus and Thrall, chelsi takes Tychus when both are open.** It denies CCS a hero they are 5-0 on, and their round two ban then falls on Thrall, which costs Hunter nothing.
4. **If Sylvanas is gone, Hunter takes Tychus and chelsi takes Thrall, in the same turn.** That locks both of chelsi's banned heroes before the round two ban and puts Hunter on his second best recent hero. Hunter's Tychus is Storm League form (20 games) with one NGS game, so it is a lean on form, not on league play. On Tomb and Infernal Shrines, where the map wants wave clear, the other option is chelsi on Tychus now and Hunter on Orphea in the next turn (CCS has never touched Orphea, so it is safe to wait).
5. **If Tychus and Thrall are both gone, chelsi goes to Tassadar (1.0 deaths) or Gul'dan, not to Sylvanas.**
6. **If they spend round one on chelsi (Thrall and Tychus, as on Dragon Shire), Junkrat or Chromie is open.** Hunter takes it (Junkrat NGS 14-0, Chromie Storm League 13-2) and chelsi takes Sylvanas only then.

Worth practicing before the match: one more hero each that CCS never bans. For chelsi, Tassadar or Gul'dan in team games (only two NGS games on each). chelsi's NGS record on offlaners (Blaze 33-17, Dehaka 26-11, Leoric 16-4) is from seasons 15 to 19, so it is old depth, not current form.

## 4. Coin toss, and first pick or map in games 2 and 3

- **CCS is 12-2 with first pick this season and 4-5 without it.** In S18 they were 6-8 with it and in S20 10-6, so this season's gap is partly a hot streak, but it is the clearest split in their games. When their opponent picked the map, CCS was 11-2.
- **The team that lost the game before chooses.** After a loss CCS took first pick 4 of 5 times (every time since our first series). Their opponents, after losing to CCS, took the map 6 of 7 times and handed CCS first pick. Across all Season 22 divisions losers took first pick 57% of the time, so CCS's habit is stronger than usual.
- The match pages do not say who won a toss, only who ended up with what. In game 1 CCS had first pick in three series (Anomaly, Cosmos in October, Roll1 in October) and the map in five, both series against us included.
- **Win the toss: take first pick.** Against us they are 2-0 with first pick and we are 2-2 when we have it.
- **Lose a game: take first pick.**
- **Win a game: expect them to take first pick, so we pick the map with second pick.** That means our map pick is the game we play from second pick (section 5 has the order).
- **Lose the toss:** expect them to take first pick, and we pick the map.

## 5. Maps

**Their map bans** (8 series): Braxis Holdout 6, Cursed Hollow 4, Alterac Pass 2, Battlefield of Eternity 2, Dragon Shire 1, Tomb 1. Their first ban has varied. **They have never banned Towers of Doom, Infernal Shrines, Sky Temple, Garden or Volskaya.** Against us they banned Braxis and Cursed Hollow, then Alterac and Braxis.

**Their map picks are not where they win:** 3-4 when they pick (Infernal Shrines 2-0, Dragon Shire 1-1, Sky Temple 0-1, Tomb 0-1, Volskaya 0-1). Against us they picked Sky Temple, Dragon Shire, Volskaya and Infernal Shrines.

**What opponents keep getting wrong:** five times a team picked Towers of Doom into them, and CCS won four.

**Our bans: Towers of Doom and Infernal Shrines.** Towers: CCS 5-1, us 0-1. Shrines: CCS 3-2 and 2-0 when they pick it, us 1-2. Do not spend a ban on Braxis: CCS bans it themselves in 6 of 8 series. Our usual Battlefield ban (5 times this season) is not needed against them.

**Our map pick (from second pick, see section 4):**
1. **Sky Temple.** We beat them there, their only game on it this season. They have never banned it.
2. **Volskaya.** We beat them there (2 deaths against 10). CCS is 1-1 on it.
3. **Garden of Terror.** We are 3-1 there this season, but it is one of the two maps they beat us on from first pick. Use the Garden plan in the cheat sheet (ban Ragnaros).
4. Tomb only with a wave clear plan (see `WAVE_CLEAR_AND_MAP_GUIDE.md`): Sylvanas is not a wave clearer, so add Orphea for Hunter or make sure chelsi's hero and the offlaner clear.

When they pick, expect Dragon Shire (they beat us there in 26 minutes), Volskaya, Sky Temple or Tomb.

## 6. What changes in games 2 and 3

**After a loss they ban the comp that beat them.** In the five series where they lost a game and played another, 9 of their 15 next game bans came from the winning comp: Junkrat and Tychus after our Sky Temple win, Brightwing and Tychus after Cosmos won Tomb, Anduin after Good Lordy won Dragon Shire, Thrall after our Volskaya win, and Tassadar, Anub'arak and Valla after Cosmos won Shrines.

So if we win game 1:
- **Whoever carried may be banned in game 2.** If it was Sylvanas, rule 4 in section 2 is the plan (Tychus for Hunter, Thrall for chelsi). They banned Sylvanas three times this season, all against Cosmos, never against us yet.
- **If Brightwing won it, it may be banned.** They did that to Cosmos's Brightwing and Good Lordy's Anduin. Ltlbearista's Anduin is 13-1 over Seasons 21 and 22 (0.4 deaths in S21), and CCS is 4-6 against teams with Brightwing or Anduin and 12-1 against every other healer. Six of their seven losses had one of the two.

If we lose game 1, expect the same bans as before (Junkrat, Chromie, then Thrall or Tychus), and take first pick.

## 8. Bans: what has and has not worked

- **Our 18 bans against them:** Sgt. Hammer 6, Qhira 4, Falstad 3, Sonya 2, Rehgar 1, Li-Ming 1, Johanna 1. No CCS player has played Sgt. Hammer or Falstad this season. That is 9 of 18 bans on heroes they do not play, and 4 more on Qhira, UnicycleYay's weakest hero (2-3, 4.0 deaths).
- **Banning their stars has not slowed them down:** across the division CCS is 6-1 when Johanna is banned, 6-1 when Qhira is banned and 4-1 when Lunara is banned. Each of their players has two or three heroes they win on.
- What their losses have in common is the other team's comp (Brightwing or Anduin) and deaths, not bans. So Johanna and Lunara stay as our round one bans because they are what CCS opens with, but the bigger lever is our own pick order.
