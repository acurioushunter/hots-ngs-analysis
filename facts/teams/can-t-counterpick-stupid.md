## Curated notes

## How they win and lose
- Deaths decide it. All four of our losses to them had 15 to 18 of our deaths. When CCS loses, every one of their players dies about twice as often (Valkamer 1.6 to 3.2, WitsEnd 1.2 to 3.2, UnicycleYay 1.6 to 3.2, ultear 1.7 to 3.5).
- Kill ultear (Gold 3) first and make WitsEnd leave Lunara. Do not chase kills on side lanes where ShadowDroid and WitsEnd can catch us.
- ShadowDroid is the clear and camp engine (5.7k minion damage a minute). Deny ShadowDroid Gazlowe.

## Healer pattern
- CCS is 4-6 against teams with Brightwing or Anduin and 12-1 against every other healer (Anomaly beat them once on Oct 14 without either). We are 2-1 with Brightwing against them and 0-3 with Rehgar or Stukov. Lock Brightwing early. It has never been banned against us.

## Qhira (UnicycleYay)
- UnicycleYay is 2-3 on Qhira with 4.0 deaths a game and 12-3 on everything else (Sylvanas, Hanzo, Tychus, Valla, Garrosh, Diablo). Do not spend a round one ban on her. The one time we did, UnicycleYay took Sylvanas and went 7/0/9.
- If she is in the draft, answer with a stun tank, Brightwing, a ranged poker, and kill her in the fights. Cosmos beat her three times that way.

## Damage first
- They spend their bans on Hunter and chelsi. Of their 18 bans against us, 15 hit a hero one of them plays: Junkrat and Chromie in round one, then Thrall or Tychus with the round two ban in 6 of 6 games. They have not banned our healer, tank or offlaner since the first game.
- We never had chelsi's Tychus or Thrall locked before that ban. chelsi on Tychus or Thrall against them: 2-1. On anything else: 0-3.
- So lock both damage players before their round two ban: slots 5 and 8 with first pick, slots 6 and 7 with second pick. Then healer, tank and offlaner. A hero already picked cannot be banned.
- They have never banned Sylvanas or Brightwing against us. Johanna is their favorite first pick (Valkamer 6-1). We ban it.

## Hunter and chelsi
- Hunter keeps Sylvanas, chelsi keeps Tychus or Thrall. When both are open chelsi takes Tychus: CCS also plays it, and their round two ban then falls on Thrall, which costs Hunter nothing.
- Sylvanas gone: Hunter on Tychus and chelsi on Thrall, both in the same turn. On wave clear maps the other option is chelsi on Tychus now and Hunter on Orphea next turn (CCS has never banned or played Orphea).
- Tychus and Thrall both gone: chelsi goes to Tassadar or Gul'dan, not to Sylvanas. If they spend round one on chelsi's heroes, Junkrat or Chromie is open for Hunter and chelsi takes Sylvanas.
- The Night board shows the pair, which heroes to lock now and which can wait.

## Coin toss, games 2 and 3
- They are 12-2 with first pick and 3-4 when they pick the map. When they have first pick against us we are 0-2. Take first pick.
- The loser of a game chooses. After a loss CCS took first pick 4 of 5 times; teams that lost to CCS took the map 6 of 7 times. So when we lose a game, take first pick. When we win one, expect them to take first pick and plan our map from second pick.
- After a loss they ban the comp that beat them: 9 of their 15 next game bans came from the winning comp. If Sylvanas carried game 1, plan for it to be banned.

## Maps
- Their map bans: Braxis Holdout in 6 of 8 series, then Cursed Hollow. They have never banned Towers of Doom, Infernal Shrines, Sky Temple, Garden or Volskaya. Ban Towers (CCS 5-1) and Shrines. Our map pick: Sky Temple, then Volskaya, then Garden.
