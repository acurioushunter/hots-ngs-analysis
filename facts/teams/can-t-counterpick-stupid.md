## Curated notes

## How they win and lose
- Deaths decide it. All four of our losses to them had 15 to 18 of our deaths. When CCS loses, every one of their players dies about twice as often (Valkamer 1.6 to 3.2, WitsEnd 1.2 to 3.2, UnicycleYay 1.6 to 3.2, ultear 1.7 to 3.5).
- Kill ultear (Gold 3) first and make WitsEnd leave Lunara. Do not chase kills on side lanes where ShadowDroid and WitsEnd can catch us.
- ShadowDroid is the clear and camp engine (5.7k minion damage a minute). Deny him Gazlowe.

## Healer pattern
- CCS is 4-6 against teams with Brightwing or Anduin and 12-1 against every other healer (Anomaly beat them once on Oct 14 without either). We are 2-1 with Brightwing against them and 0-3 with Rehgar or Stukov. Lock Brightwing early. It has never been banned against us.

## Qhira (UnicycleYay)
- He is 2-3 on Qhira with 4.0 deaths a game and 12-3 on everything else (Sylvanas, Hanzo, Tychus, Valla, Garrosh, Diablo). Do not spend a round one ban on her. The one time we did, he took Sylvanas and went 7/0/9.
- If she is in the draft, answer with a stun tank, Brightwing, a ranged poker, and kill her in the fights. Cosmos beat her three times that way.

## Draft timing
- They ban Junkrat and Chromie in round one (slots 1 to 4) and Thrall and Tychus in round two (slot 10 or 11). A hero already picked cannot be banned. Lock Thrall or Tychus by slot 9 (first pick) or slot 7 (second pick).
- They have never banned Sylvanas or Brightwing against us.
- Johanna is their favorite first pick (Valkamer 6-1) and counters Tychus and Brightwing. We ban it.

## Coin toss
- They are 12-2 with first pick and 3-4 when they pick the map. When they have first pick against us we are 0-2. Take first pick.

## Their Garden of Terror comp
- Qhira, Anub'arak, Li-Ming, Tyrande and Ragnaros beat us on fights (5.0k against 3.5k fight damage a minute) and experience (3.33k against 2.69k). Ban Ragnaros. Chen and Stukov answer Ragnaros, Leoric or Varian answer Anub'arak.
