### About the account trading suspicion

What the data says, with its limits:

- **Their accounts:** ten different accounts across 17 games. All ten have Storm League MMR between 2550 (Silver 2) and 2814 (Platinum 1). None looks like a much higher ranked account.
- **The first game against us (Aug 26):** Batlin, Hiscabibbel, YataGarasu, moon and Edawg187. Edawg187 has only played those 2 games for them. I checked three of them. Edawg187 has 1,215 Storm League games on Chromie and 164 on Muradin, and YataGarasu has 101 on Sylvanas at 68%, so both played heroes they know well. moon played Leoric (87 games) and Zul'jin (8 games), a thinner history. That fits a normal team picking comfort heroes.
- **moon:** 2,531 Storm League games, 882 on The Lost Vikings at 55%. In NGS he averages rating 68.6, about 10.8 above what his MMR predicts, in the top 4 of 284 players with 8 or more games (CCS's ShadowDroid is in the same group). That is unusual but not on its own evidence of anything.
- **The mixed results afterwards:** ratings swing from 40s to 70s between games for the same players. That fits a team playing well and badly, and it does not point to a pattern.

Nothing here shows who was at the keyboard, and Heroes Profile cannot show that. I would not accuse them. If your team wants to raise it, the person to ask is an NGS admin and they would need more than a performance feeling. For prep it does not change anything: plan for moon at his best and keep Leoric available.
