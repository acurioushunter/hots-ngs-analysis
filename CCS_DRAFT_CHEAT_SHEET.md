# CCS draft cheat sheet (one page of decisions)

Everything here comes from `CCS_MATCH_PREP.md`, `COMP_AND_POKE_INSIGHTS.md`, `WAVE_CLEAR_AND_MAP_GUIDE.md` and `RECENT_FORM_AND_POOLS.md`. Small samples, so these are leans. Read the section for your situation, ignore the rest.

## 0. What CCS does (the facts the plan is built on)
- Bans against us: **Junkrat and Chromie in round one** (slots 1 to 4), **Thrall and Tychus in round two** (slot 10 or 11). Junkrat 5 of 6 games, Thrall 5, Chromie 3, Tychus 2.
- A hero we have **already picked** cannot be banned in round two. So Thrall and Tychus must be locked by **slot 9** (first pick) or **slot 7** (second pick).
- Their first picks: Johanna 4 times (Valkamer 5-1 on it), Sylvanas 2 (UnicycleYay 3-1), Qhira 2. Their best hero is Lunara (WitsEnd 8-3). Their carry is ShadowDroid (Gazlowe 3-1, Dehaka 4-2).
- They are **9-2 with first pick, 3-4 when they pick the map.** We are 0-2 against them, both 1-2, and lost all four losses with 15+ deaths.
- Johanna counters our Tychus and Brightwing. Facing two or more marksmen with one or none of ours is 5-12 for teams in this division. Their marksmen are Lunara, Sylvanas (UnicycleYay), Hanzo, Tychus.

## 1. The coin toss
**If we win it: take first pick, let them have the map.**
- It keeps first pick away from a team that is 9-2 with it, and when CCS picks the map they are only 3-4 (2-2 against us).
- It lets us take Sylvanas at slot 5, before UnicycleYay can.
- Our own numbers lean the other way (6-2 as map picker, 6-6 as first pick, small samples). If you would rather have the map, use Section 3. The cost is that they open the draft, so plan for Johanna or Sylvanas being gone.
**If we lose it:** CCS will almost certainly take first pick, so we are second pick and the map is ours. Use Section 3.

## 4. Who can play what (the swap tool)
Because you can pick a hero and decide later who plays it, take the contested one first.
| Hero | Hunter | chelsi | Use it |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Sylvanas** | 58% over 119 SL games, NGS 6-4 | 5-2 in 7 HealsOnly games | Slot 5 or 6. Most flexible hero, and denies UnicycleYay |
| **Chromie** | 12-3 SL last year, NGS 1-1 | NGS 2-1, 1-0 HealsOnly | The other swap hero. CCS bans it in round one about half the time |
| **Tychus** | not proven in my notes | NGS 5-2 | chelsi only unless you want to test it |
| **Orphea** | 12-5 SL, NGS S21 3-0 | no data | Hunter's backup, CCS has never banned it |
| **Thrall** | no | 7-2 HealsOnly, NGS 3-0 | chelsi only, lock by slot 8 or 9 |
| Tassadar, Gul'dan | no | Tassadar clears 5.5k a minute with 1.0 deaths, Gul'dan 5.4k damage but 3.0 deaths | chelsi's fallback damage |
**Rule of thumb:** pick Sylvanas first. Whichever of Hunter and chelsi does not have a good second hero after the bans, swap so that Hunter gets the better of Sylvanas and Orphea and chelsi gets Thrall or Tychus.

Always check: **2 real damage dealers**, **2 or more marksmen if we can**, and a ranged clearer on Tomb. The other mistake from the Tomb draft was ending on melee with one ranged hero.

## 6b. Qhira (UnicycleYay)
Full detail in `QHIRA_DEEP_DIVE.md`. Short version: **do not ban Qhira in round one.** UnicycleYay is 2-3 on Qhira (4.0 deaths a game) and **8-2 on everything else** (Sylvanas 3-1, Hanzo 2-1, Tychus 2-0, Valla 1-0). The one time we banned it first and let Sylvanas go, he went 7/0/9 and we lost. Deny his marksmen instead: Sylvanas is ours at slot 5, a late ban on **Hanzo** (his highest damage hero) if it is open. If he does take Qhira, draft the Cosmos shape that beat her three times: a frontline with a stun, Brightwing or Rehgar, a ranged poker, and kill her in the fights.

## 6c. Healer and Garden lessons (details in `CCS_FULL_PICTURE.md`)
- CCS is **3-6 against Brightwing or Anduin teams and 9-0 against any other healer**, and we are 2-1 with Brightwing against them and 0-3 with Rehgar or Stukov. **Lock Brightwing early** (slot 9 on first pick with Thrall at 8, slot 7 on second pick if Thrall is already ours), and put the tank after it.
- **Garden:** three ranged heroes is the 1-4 shape. Their Garden comp is Qhira, Anub'arak, Li-Ming, Tyrande and Ragnaros. Ban Ragnaros before Sonya. Chen (chelsi) and Stukov (Ltlbearista) are the in pool answers to Ragnaros, Leoric or Varian answer Anub'arak.
- If E.T.C. is the Qhira answer, give him a partner: he was 1/6/7 alone in the front.

## 7. In game, not in the draft
- **Deaths decide this series.** We lost every game where we died 15 or more times and won both where we died 7 or fewer.
- Kill **ûltear** (Gold 3) first and make WitsEnd leave Lunara. Do not chase kills on the side lanes where ShadowDroid and WitsEnd can catch us.
- Do not let UnicycleYay sit on Sylvanas, and do not give ShadowDroid Gazlowe.
