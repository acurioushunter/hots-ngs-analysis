# Draft tips

## How the NGS draft runs
- Slots 1 to 4 are bans. The first pick team bans 1 and 3, the other team bans 2 and 4.
- Slots 5 to 9 are picks. The first pick team takes 5, 8 and 9. The other team takes 6 and 7.
- Slots 10 and 11 are the second round of bans. The other team bans 10, the first pick team bans 11.
- Slots 12 to 16 are picks. The other team takes 12, 13 and 16. The first pick team takes 14 and 15.
- The coin toss winner chooses first pick or the map pick. The team that does not have first pick also gets the last pick (slot 16).
- Each team ends with 3 bans and 5 picks. Every ban and pick on the Draft tab is tied to its slot number.

## Bans
- General practice: ban what the other team needs, not what scares you. Check the player's own record on the hero first. The Prep tab marks heroes a player is clearly worse on than on his others, so banning them may do nothing.
- Round one bans (1 to 4) go on heroes that decide the game on any map, or on the hero a team opens with. The tool weighs how much they rely on a hero, their first pick habit and the map tier.
- Round two bans (10 and 11) answer what is already on the board: the hero that completes their comp, or the counter to what we have picked.
- If the other team bans a hero of ours late (the Prep tab lists these), lock that hero early. A late ban cannot take what is already picked.
- Do not ban a hero in our own pools unless a rule says so. The tool already skips them.

## Picks
- General practice: early picks (5 to 7) should be heroes that are good on this map and hard to counter, or heroes the other team would take away with a late ban.
- Keep one or two flexible picks for the middle of the draft. A hero that can fill two roles lets you wait to see their comp.
- Take the healer and the front line before the board runs out of good ones. A missing healer or tank is hard to fix with the last two picks.
- Last picks (14 to 16) are for the gap: what the comp is missing, or the counter to what they just locked. Look at the comp meter on the Draft tab before you choose.
- The Icy Veins counter and synergy lists are a starting point, not a rule. Read the hero page for the reason before you pick on it.

## What the comp meter checks
- **Ranged count.** Our own numbers on each map decide how many ranged heroes we want. Blaze is never counted as ranged. See the rules on the Prep tab.
- **Marksmen.** Auto attack ranged heroes such as Greymane, Valla, Raynor, Tychus, Lunara, Hanzo, Cassia, Sylvanas, Zul'jin, Fenix and Falstad.
- **Front line and healer.** At least one of each, and the map page says when two healers or two bruisers are preferred.
- **Wave clear.** A hero that clears a wave quickly lets the team rotate to an objective without losing a lane.
- **Damage dealers.** Heroes that average 2,400 or more hero damage a minute in this division.

## Reading the other team
- Count their ranged heroes, marksmen and wave clear, and compare with ours on the comp meter. Several Icy Veins map pages list wave clear as a priority, so check the map page.
- Look at their healer. The Prep tab shows their record against each healer and who they struggle against.
- Look for a hero that only works in a pairing (Icy Veins pair notes are on the hero pages under Matchups).
- Note who they gave their carry to. Players often have one or two heroes they are much better on.

## After the draft
- Open the Maps tab for the lane formation and the objective plan. Every map page has the opening and the win conditions from Icy Veins.
- Learn tab, Opening moves: group, siege, gank, trap or split. Decide before the gates open.
- Open each hero page for the build. Check the date on every build code and the flag when the talents page changed after the code was made.
- Copy the draft summary from the end of the Draft tab and post it in your team chat for the post match review.

## Limits of this tool
- Match records are small samples. Most are 1 to 12 games, so read them as leans, not rules.
- The NGS match pages have no timeline, so nothing here knows who was ahead at ten minutes.
- Icy Veins pages carry their own update dates. A page that has not changed since 2020 can describe a hero that has since changed.
