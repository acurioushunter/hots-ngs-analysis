# Map strategy (written 8 Oct 2026)

Limits: the exact current Storm League / NGS map pool was NOT found. Haunted Mines returned to Ranked on 20 Jul 2026. [source: https://patchbot.io/games/heroes-of-the-storm] A Boostroom guide lists 16 battlegrounds, below. Spawn timers were not given by any readable current source, so none are invented here. Check project playbook map pages (from Icy Veins, already in the repo) for NGS lane and comp notes; this file adds only what is new.

Main sources: B = [source: https://boostroom.com/blog/heroes-of-the-storm-maps-guide-objectives-strategies-and-battleground-tips], D = [source: https://dignitas.gg/articles/blogs/Heroes-Of-The-Storm/13549/heroes-of-the-storm-objective-and-camp-control-in-infernal-shrines-volskaya-foundry-alterac-pass-and-towers-of-doom] (about 2019, numbers may be stale).

## Alterac Pass
- Objective: free prisoners (kill the tanky minions, then channel and hold); cavalry in all three lanes, push buff of 30 percent move speed and 10 percent damage and spell power (D).
- Timing: take the siege camp as soon as it spawns and again before the objective; it respawns roughly every 90 seconds mid game (D, may be stale).
- One player is enough to hold the channel; guards do not interrupt like heroes do, so others can take XP or camps and return (D).
- Comp: global heroes, wave clear, mercenary control, poke and area control (B).
- Mistakes: collapsing side lanes while grouped at the prison; starting the objective while behind in XP or talents (B). Overextending for forts early; failing to finish the core once weak, since it regenerates; with two keeps left, defend keeps over core (D).

## Battlefield of Eternity
- Objective: two Immortals; each team races the enemy one. The winner's Immortal pushes a lane (B).
- Comp: strong PvE damage to win the race, or superior team fighting to pull the enemy off their Immortal (B).
- Mistake: committing without checking that you win the damage race (B).

## Blackheart's Bay
- Objective: collect doubloons from chests and camps, turn in to Blackheart; paid ship bombards enemy structures. Carried doubloons drop on death. Required amount starts at 10 and rises as the ship fires (B).
- Rotation: protect the carrier, punish a heavily loaded enemy, turn in safely (B).
- Mistake: chasing kills while carrying a big stack [judgment, matches Tomb advice in B].

## Braxis Holdout
- Objective: two beacons; both teams get Zerg waves, winner gets the stronger one (B).
- Comp: sustain, duelers, displacement, reliable control, strong solo lane (B).
- Mistake: panicking when the enemy briefly holds both beacons; dying alone at a beacon; leaving a lane open (B).

## Cursed Hollow
- Objective: three tributes curse the enemy: minions drop to one health, structures stop firing (B).
- Comp: global mobility, fast rotations, wave clear, independent offlaners (B).
- Mistakes: losing several heroes to contest an early tribute (giving it up is often better); failing to convert a curse into lasting structure damage (B). The third tribute matters most (B).

## Dragon Shire
- Objective: hold both shrines to activate the Dragon Knight (B).
- Comp: strong rotations and offlane play rather than a single type (B).
- Mistake: sending all five to the center with neither shrine controlled; if they commit to one shrine, pressure the other (B).

## Garden of Terror
- Objective: three seeds spawn a Garden Terror per lane; Overgrowth disables enemy structures (B).
- Patch note: Shambler health and damage start 25 percent lower and grow back (28 Sep 2026 patch; PTR source). [source: https://jeu.video/article/heroes-of-the-storm-xal-atath-im-ptr-mit-balance-aenderungen-fuer-sieben-helden]
- Play: push with the Terrors and use disabled structures; when defending, destroy the Overgrowth (B). Three lane pressure matters (B).

## Hanamura Temple
- Objective: stand near the contested payload to move it; delivery fires a barrage at enemy structures. Sentinel camp adds pushing pressure (B).
- Comp: heroes who hold space around the payload and give merc pressure (B).
- Mistake: ignoring the Sentinel (B).

## Haunted Mines
- Objective: defeat the undead army in the mine for skulls; both teams get a Grave Golem and the one with more skulls gets the stronger one (B).
- 11 May 2026 balance: skull scaling 5 to 7 percent per skull, early Golem scaling lowered, late Golem scaling significantly raised. [source: https://news.blizzard.com/en-us/article/24276959/heroes-of-the-storm-balance-patch-notes-may-11-2026] (Another outlet described a skull cap change; unconfirmed, do not use.)
- Play: do not die for small Golem upgrades; do not abandon every lane or the enemy gets value above ground (B).
- Comp: frontline and sustain read as better after May (secondary commentary). [source: https://www.gamer.org/heroes-of-the-storms-may-11-balance-patch-hits-haunted-mines-buffs-tanks-and-trims-deathwing/] Late game mine fights matter more than before [judgment from the scaling change].

## Infernal Shrines
- Objective: shrines summon guardians; first to 40 gets a Punisher (D).
- Timing: first shrine about 30 seconds in, weak (D). Shrine type: purple Arcane, orange Mortar, blue Frost (D). Later shrines spawn in new places.
- Comp: AoE that clears guardians; line skillshots get intercepted (D).
- Camps: kill the top lane Shaman first or it keeps respawning dogs (D). Take siege camps early (D).
- Tricks: the 39 of 40 hold (leave the shrine at 39 and claim it when ready) when the enemy is far behind; Punisher jump has about a 5 second first cooldown then 15 (D, may be stale). Do not fight over a weak first Punisher (D).
- Defense: stay back from structures since Punishers leap and stun (B).

## Sky Temple
- Objective: temples activate periodically, holding one fires at enemy structures (B).
- Play: you do not need every temple; trading one is fine; do not fight badly over a single temple (B). Interval not given.

## Tomb of the Spider Queen
- Objective: minions drop gems; turning in enough summons a Webweaver in each lane (B).
- Comp: wave clear and efficient four player rotations (B).
- Mistakes: hoarding gems; chasing kills with a large stack (B).

## Towers of Doom
- Objective: cores start at 40 health, altars fire at the core, damage rises with bell towers held (B).
- Early: sapper camps; the bottom lane is key; top camp supports the solo lane (D).
- Timing: altar channel 6 seconds; later altars spawn 110 seconds after the last capture (D, may be stale). Poke channelers to interrupt (D).
- Late: bottom sapper camps and both bottom bell towers; six towers held damages the core over time (D).
- Mistakes: ignoring the bottom lane; treating altar shots as minor (D). Core health is a resource (B).

## Volskaya Foundry
- Objective: complete the large capture point to get the Triglav Protector (B). Points cycle middle, top, bottom; about a minute to capture (6 seconds to take, 54 of progress); one hero can hold; contest to deny or push into overtime at 99 percent (D, may be stale).
- Camps: fortification camps give XP and turrets; the support camp gives Healing Pulse (40 percent team health and mana over 10 seconds), take it with a lead (D).
- Conveyors: move with the belt (D).
- Protector: pilot W on towers, Q charge, E shields; gunner W on structures; pull the tank back if the enemy engages (D). Players in it lose their kit, so choose who goes in (B).
- Comp: sustain and positioning (B). Mistakes: overextending for towers; skipping fountain taps (D).

## Warhead Junction
- Objective: collect warheads, launch at a target area. Interrupted launch penalty reduced to 5 seconds from 10 after the July 2026 update (B; date unverified). Slime boss exists.
- Comp: global heroes, strong solo laners, players who track missing enemies (B).
- Mistakes: walking alone with a warhead; aiming off center does not add damage, aim at the most valuable structures (B).
