Where they disagree with the Icy Veins text or an agent's reading, his note wins unless the league data clearly contradicts it (then say both). Spelling and wording are left as he wrote it. ## Bruisers and tanks
Dehaka is correct a bruiser who clears waves and tries to offlane before obj to pull the other offlaner away then burrow into obj with team swinging a 4v4 into a 5v4 with his global, or keep pushing while his team stalls if he's not contested in lane by the other offlaner, even better if they time a camp to push with him.

Deathwing is a bruiser, often taken in the 4 man as a dmg dealer. You try to pick him if the other team doesn't have a % damage hero and if they have a pick comp since he cant be stunned he destroys certain comps and is a really strong late pick hero in the right situations.

D.va is a bruiser, she is focused on soaking lanes and controlling points with her bombs or disrupting fights with bunnyhop etc.

Varian is a tank if he picks taunt at 4. He is a bruiser/melee assassin if he takes twin blades or colossus smash at 4.

Thrall is a bruiser/melee assassin who has decent sustain and damage but isnt as fast at clearing waves or taking or defending camps.

Sonya is a good brawler bruiser like thrall. She is better at wave clear and camps, she has high burst and can jump the enemy backline if she goes leap, often a good hero for combos like light bomb or other cc chain combos. If she takes her other ult she has higher sustain for fights, if the enemy doesnt have cc (crowd control) she can use her spinning ability I think its her E to deal decent damage and sustain through lots of fights.

Malthael is a bruiser who is great at camps, double soaking, fighting high hp heroes, and in team fights if they don't have cc to pin him down or reduce healing abilities he can sustain enough to kill a team, really weak to cc burst damage and reduced healing though and doesnt have any cc or escapes.

Ragnaros is a bruiser who soaks lanes well and can use his trait to defend against storng enemy push or defend near obj to stall them. His smash ult when set up with cc before can do huge damage or his lava wave can clear heavy enemy push. He is good on most wave clear maps but on tomb of the spider queen if he kills waves with lava wave you lose those gems so a small thing to keep in mind. He also doesnt have a lot of health or an escape so he loses against other bruisers that can cc him or out sustain him.

## Ranged and mages
Hanzo does a lot of poke damage. its a mix of spell and auto dmg and minions or summons can block the abilities. He is also skill shot dependant. he can jump over tarain to escape in a lot of situations but high mobility dive heroes like zeratul or genji can easily kill him. His dragon arrow ult can be used as engage for big team fights or disengage mostly trying to use it in choke points or when the enemy team is clumped up. He has low health and is killed easily. He doesnt excell at camps or wave clear, but can talent into decent obj or boss dmg with his w lvl 4 talent.

Junkrat is a poke sustain dmg hero, he doesnt have much burst outside of his rip tire ult. His main use is disrupting the enemy team and controlling areas with W bomb and E traps. He has good escape, wave clear, camp clear. No % dmg and not a fast boss clear, but a solid hero on certain maps and comps.

Kel'Thuzad is a high skill floor burst mage, he does best if you have easy cc to set up his combos. He does massive burst damage but isnt very popular as he doesnt have escapes and its hard to hit his combos to be effective.

Lunara is a sustain dps, she doesnt have much burst damage but can kite and poke heroes well with her slightly higher move speed in lane and ranged autos. She can't mount so is stuck at her movement speed which is higher then base speed but I think lower than mounted speed for other heroes. She does great against low healing supports like uther because if she can apply her poison to many people he doesnt have the sustain or mana to keep up with her damamge, but for other healers like brightwing they have consistent damage that can negate her sustain damage.

Orphea is a burst mage. she is medium range and has a high skill ceiling since she has to get close to heroes and is weak if she misses her q. lots of dmg, some boss and area control with her ult that resets if it hits a hero which can be used well if you have a mosh ult from etc or a leoric entomb or other solid cc.

Sgt hammer is a ranged sustain dps who can seige from very far but is suseptible to dive or long range poke like tassadar chromie maybe li ming. if the team doesn draft to kill her she can destroy buildings and dominate in team fights but very dependant on draft and positioning since she cant reposition quickly and can get locked down by cc if her unstoppable talent on shiled is down or trait is down or interupted etc.

Nazeebo is a sustain dmg mage, with some burst from his razenous spirit ult or spider build. He has no escapes other than a late game talent ice block which just delays doesnt move him out of fights. He is skill shot dependant and weak in competative but stronger against uncordinated teams. Good at wave clear and camp clear though.

Probius is setup dependant because he needs pylons around him, can get killed from dive or cc and weaker in the meta now.

Nova is a burst assassin ranged dps who is focused on getting pick kills. weaker hero without much sustain but can be strong if the player is good with her and ganks the offlaner or vulnerable heroes.

Genji is a dive burst assassin who can burst down a vulnerable backline. He can escape well but is susceptible to cc and has low hp.

Falstad is a sustain dps with a global trait. He tries to side lane and stack with auto lvl 1 quest. if he gets stacks he does lots of dps and has a late game % dmg auto trait. He can also go w but suseptible to getting caught if they have cc to punish that, or can go q for poke dmg.

Fenix is medium range sustain dps. can solo like raynor but often in 3 or 4 man but catches waves and ganks like a sylv or ray greymane etc.

Azmodan is a burst ranged dps who sieges structures well but is cooldown dependant so big burst than not much damage no escapes.

## Melee assassins
Valeera is similar to nova in that they try to get isolated picks or ganks. She can be good but struggles against coordinated teams unless the valerra player is really good at timing engages, genrally weaker at team fights and stronger in laning phase.

Illidan is a dive melee assassin with decent sustain and can dive backlines hard but is weak to cc. He is a lane bully against a lot of heroes and has a high skill ceiling if the player is good.

Samuro also has a high skill ceiling. He can offlane well and later can double soak. He can be hard to kill so thats his main strength like pulling attention from objectives so his team can cap them and wasting the enemies time if they move people to gank and he still escapes.

Murky is a split pusher with low death timer if his egg is safe. He can get octo grab to pick people and similar to samuro in that he wants to push side lanes and is best if he gets siege and structre damage while pulling attention and escaping. He struggles against ragnaros or others who can kill him for stacks or quests or kill his w so that it doesnt proc.

## Healers and supports
Stukov is a healer who is a mix of sustain and burst healing but doesn't excel at either. His main strength is his aoe channeled silence. He doesnt have a solid escape other then his swipes ult to push people back, and can be focused by dive if his team doesnt have a solid frontline to defend him. When drafted with taunt varian or another easy to hit CC tank he silences under the target and you try to focus down the taunted target with a burst damage ranged assassin like jaina since she can easily drop all her damage quickly and it isn't blocked by minions or enemy heroes. Other burst dmg can also work like greymane, li ming, kel, thuzard, Keal thas etc. He struggles if the other team has a way to save the target he and varain focus, like anduin pull can counter it easily. You can also counter it with medivh or uther divine shield or tyreals invulnerable ability, cleanses also help but the stukov silence can still lead to kills even if the target is cleansed.

Tyrande is a sustain healer, but has to auto to lower her cooldown on heal so it depends on being able to safely auto. She is often picked as a follow up to a CC tank to focus down a single target with some burst ranged dps or melee assassin.

Whitemane is pretty srting at the moment. She doesn't have much cc herself or an escape but has high burst healing. She needs to damage heroes to increase the heals for heroes marked with her q though. She is weak to tanks who can pull or seperate her from her team like stitches, garrash, diablo. But also mixed against dive, she can survive the dive if she is able to damage them to do massive burst healing. Overall a strong healer at this time, but takes skill and good positioning, since she doesnt have much cc its good to draft that in the tank and maybe the offlaner.

Rehgar is a healer, he has okay heals but nothing massive until he gets his ancestral healing which is on a long cooldown. He is good at camp and wave clear. He can slow heroes with his totem, he has a trait that can cleanse an ally or slow a enemy but it has a long cooldown, and he cant use the trait on himself and also no escape abilities. He is good if team is lacking wave clear but weak in other areas, overall a decent healer.

Lucio is another good healer he doesnt have great healing numbers compared to some but is solid in sustain and can pick a shiled ult to deal with burst dmg or high five to cleanse cc from allies. He has a high skill floor and ceiling. If played well he can disrput rotations and be hard to kill do to his mobility but some heroes like diablo can use that to their advantage and stun him on walls if he's wall riding.

Khara is good for dive comps since he has lots of burst dmg. high skill ceiling and not much healing if he cant auto stuff.

Li li is okay at sustain healing and has a cleanse but no escapes solid in low ranks but not as effective high ranked or competative.

Auriel needs to damamge people or have a teammate with her trait on then dealing dmg to fill her heals. she can do decent dmg sustain heal in some comps and weak in others. no solid escape.

Anti heal: ana has her anti heal grenade, varian has an anit heal talent so does johanna etc.
