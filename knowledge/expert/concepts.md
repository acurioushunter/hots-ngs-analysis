# Macro concepts (written 8 Oct 2026)

Sourcing note: sourced lines come from a Dignitas mercenary camp article, a Boostroom Aug 2026 ranked guide, a Blizzard beginner post and a search summary of Icy Veins forum "Midgame moves" content. Dignitas pieces are around 2018 to 2019, so numbers may have changed. Everything else is [judgment] (standard coaching content, not checked against a page).

## Shared experience and soak
- Experience is shared, so the whole team levels together; soaking is a team job, not a selfish one. [source: https://mail.infosecinstitute.com/heroes-of-the-storm-guides-1423105530.html] (search summary, low quality source)
- Soak means standing in a lane near enemy minions so the team gets XP without anyone dying. One hero in each of three lanes early. [source: https://news.blizzard.com/en-us/article/24242866/five-tips-for-beginners]
- The XP unlocks talent tiers. Level 10 (Heroic) is the big threshold. Do not fight behind in talent tiers. [source: https://boostroom.com/blog/heroes-of-the-storm-ranked-guide-how-storm-league-works-and-how-to-climb]
- Double soaking (two heroes in the same lane) is used when the enemy leaves a lane or to stack a quest (example: Nazeebo and an offlaner on Volskaya). [source: https://dignitas.gg/articles/blogs/Heroes-Of-The-Storm/13549/heroes-of-the-storm-objective-and-camp-control-in-infernal-shrines-volskaya-foundry-alterac-pass-and-towers-of-doom]
- Bruisers should leave the lane briefly to join the team after level 10 or so, acting as secondary engage or peel. A Reddit opinion via a search summary; treat as [judgment].

## Rotations
- Rotate to the objective as a group with waves cleared or being cleared by a wave clear hero. Do not leave a lane to collapse while the team gathers (Alterac note). [source: https://boostroom.com/blog/heroes-of-the-storm-maps-guide-objectives-strategies-and-battleground-tips]
- Rotations need wave clear: that is why wave clear is a map priority on many pages [judgment, also in draft_tips.md].
- A missing enemy is information: if one lane hero vanishes, assume a rotation, ping and keep the team together [judgment].

## Camp timing and tempo
- Mercenary camps give little XP alone; value is in timing. [source: https://dignitas.gg/articles/blogs/Heroes-Of-The-Storm/11768/heroes-of-the-storm-how-to-fully-utilize-mercenary-camps]
- Best window: clear camps just before an objective spawns, so the enemy has to choose between defending the camp and the objective. Same source.
- Boss camps: take them with the team and push the lane together. Same source.
- Siege and bruiser camps: do not grab them whenever free; wait for a major objective or for the enemy to leave the lane. Same source.
- Mistake: camping right after a successful objective push. Your lanes are already pushed into enemy territory; go back, let lanes reset. Same source.
- Mistake: taking a camp while several enemies are in the lane. Same source.
- Tempo means who forces the next decision. The team that sets up the next objective with camps, vision and cooldowns ready usually has tempo [judgment].

## Objective stacking and trading
- Objectives are not optional in HotS, and timing often outweighs lane pressure. [source: https://mail.infosecinstitute.com/heroes-of-the-storm-guides-1423105530.html] (low quality; also standard practice [judgment])
- Stacking objectives means using a lead from one objective (lanes pushed, camps taken, forts down) to be ready for the next. Forts and towers lost make later objective hits more dangerous (Sky Temple note). [source: https://boostroom.com/blog/heroes-of-the-storm-maps-guide-objectives-strategies-and-battleground-tips]
- Trading: giving up an objective for something bigger is fine. Examples in the sources: give up an early Tribute rather than lose several heroes on Cursed Hollow; give a Sky Temple rather than take a bad fight; Towers of Doom core health is a resource. Same Boostroom source.
- Do not die for small upgrades (Haunted Mines Golem note). Same source.
- Convert fights into objectives, structures or camps, not into chasing one low health survivor. [source: https://boostroom.com/blog/heroes-of-the-storm-ranked-guide-how-storm-league-works-and-how-to-climb]

## Level lead
- A level lead (especially hitting 10 first) is strongest when you force fights where you have ults and they do not; a community opinion via search summary. [source: https://cal1.lr.ggtyler.dev/r/heroesofthestorm/comments/1kf8gqc/bruiser_problem/mqt4am8/?context=3] (Reddit mirror, opinion)
- Level leads shrink late as talents run out; around level 20 the gap matters less than ult status, positioning and death timers [judgment].
- Use the lead aggressively: take camps and objective early, deny soak, force the trailing team to fight at a disadvantage [judgment].
- When behind, avoid even fights; play for waves, safe camps and a defended objective until a spike. Boostroom says the same: avoid fights behind in talent tiers.

## Ult economy
- Heroics and ults are on long cooldowns, so tracking them is a core skill at Gold and above. [source: https://boostroom.com/blog/heroes-of-the-storm-ranked-guide-how-storm-league-works-and-how-to-climb]
- Take fights when your key ults are up and theirs are down; if their best ult (for example Void Prison style CC or a big AoE) just went off, that is your window [judgment].
- Do not waste heroics on a fight you were going to win or lose anyway; hold for the objective fight [judgment].
- Respawn timers are shorter since April 2026 (late game), so a won fight gives a shorter window to push. [source: https://patchbot.io/games/heroes-of-the-storm]

## Shot calling
- One voice calls the plan; others report info. Say: "objective in 30 seconds, rotate now, camps first". [judgment]
- The game warns 30 seconds before the next objective on all battlegrounds (a 2018 note; may have changed). [source: https://news.blizzard.com/fr-fr/article/21295033/2018]
- Decide before the gates open: group, siege, gank, trap or split (see playbook notes). [judgment]
- Call targets before the fight and call off when it goes wrong. Do not blame the draft in chat; it hurts the team. [source: https://boostroom.com/blog/heroes-of-the-storm-ranked-guide-how-storm-league-works-and-how-to-climb]
- After a fight, the caller says what to convert: objective, fort, camp or reset. [judgment]
