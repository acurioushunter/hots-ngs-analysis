# Hero insights (written 8 Oct 2026)

Honest limit: reachable sources are mostly GosuGamers editorials from about 2015 (the pages show "11 years ago"), Blizzard forum threads and a 2026 Boostroom guide. Hero kits have been reworked many times since, so treat every 2015 claim as a hypothesis to check against the current hero page and Hunter's data. Icy Veins hero pages and the Hero Synergies Guide (updated June 2025 per a search result) returned 403 and were not read. Most of this file is therefore [judgment] and flagged as such.

## Hidden or underrated strengths (dated, check first)
- Zeratul: no weak build, stealth makes enemies cautious; Void Prison called the best ultimate in the game in 2015. [source: https://www.gosugamers.net/heroesofthestorm/features/38316-unsung-underrated-the-heroes-we-don-t-respect-but-should] Void Prison's current strength unverified.
- Nazeebo: crowd control, works in push and teamfight comps, thoughtful ult choices. Same source (2015).
- Nova: can delete a chosen target before a fight and forces enemies to play cautious; Crippling Shot at 16 was said to counter tanks. Same source (2015, talent may have changed).
- Gazlowe: swings between carry and useless depending on talents; defensive and teamfight talents give steady damage and slows. Same source. 11 May 2026 buff: Rock-it Turret 37 to 43, One Man Wrecking Crew 60 to 75 percent. [source: https://news.blizzard.com/en-us/article/24276959/heroes-of-the-storm-balance-patch-notes-may-11-2026]
- Azmodan: controls siege objectives from range, big late globe; weak to body blocking and lockdown. 2015 source above.
- Fenix: described as underrated for flexible build paths and two solid ults (Blizzard forum). [source: https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/heroes/t/whats-the-most-underratedoverrated-heroes-in-the-game/55101]
- Abathur: strong at split pushing and global support; also overrated in Quick Match because players do not place mines or play proactively. Same forum source and a tier list snippet. [judgment on how this carries to NGS]
- Arthas and Chen: 2026 buffs make them worth rechecking on tight choke maps (commentary, not data). [source: https://www.gamer.org/heroes-of-the-storms-may-11-balance-patch-hits-haunted-mines-buffs-tanks-and-trims-deathwing/]

## Combos and synergies (2015 article unless stated)
[source: https://www.gosugamers.net/heroesofthestorm/news/38368-8-hero-synergies-that-dominate-the-current-meta]
- Tyrande plus Jaina: roam early, focus one target, burst it; any hero with Tyrande gives early gank pressure.
- Johanna plus Azmodan: Johanna protects him while he farms Taste for Blood stacks and sets up his dunks.
- Malfurion plus Kael'thas: Malfurion's heals beat other poke; they win poke wars. Weakness: Kael can be burst faster than he can be healed.
- Johanna with the Abathur hat: strong early map control; with Illidan or another melee assassin sets up level 20 Hivemind plays.
- Nazeebo plus Malfurion: Zombie Wall sets up Entangling Roots; damage and healing over time grind comps down.
- Zeratul plus a Divine Shield user (Uther): shield lets Zeratul auto attack safely.
- Tassadar plus Illidan was called very strong but hard to play (forum, via search snippet). [source: https://www.icy-veins.com/forums/topic/22666-best-duo-lane-comps/]
- Lt. Morales: lets a team overextend for kills; Sonya and Greymane named as pairs (forum snippet, same link). Current status unverified.
- Muradin plus Tyrande: stun lock duo; Tyrande's partner must apply real pressure. Same forum thread.
- Illidan with Abathur symbiote: Adrenal Overload at 4 helps dives. Snippet source; talent may have changed.

## Anti synergies and cautions
- Fragile ranged damage without protection: Valla wants a second healer or support. [source: https://www.icy-veins.com/heroes/valla] (snippet only)
- Mephisto: avoid unnecessary teamfights and spread. Garrosh: punishes heroes who get close early. [source: https://icy-veins.com/heroes/tier-list] (snippet; 2026 status unverified)
- Two heroes that both need to be protected (Kael'thas plus Azmodan, or any two squishy sieges) are anti synergy because the front line cannot cover both [judgment].
- A comp of only dive heroes with no wave clear loses to a siege comp without a fight [judgment; see draft_theory.md].
- Line skillshots on Infernal Shrines get eaten by guardians. [source: https://dignitas.gg/articles/blogs/Heroes-Of-The-Storm/13549/heroes-of-the-storm-objective-and-camp-control-in-infernal-shrines-volskaya-foundry-alterac-pass-and-towers-of-doom]

## Misplays by role (no single Heroes specific public source found; generic lists from Boostroom, rest [judgment])
- All roles: playing too many heroes and never learning matchups; fighting while behind in talent tiers; chasing a low health enemy after a won fight; copying builds without understanding them. [source: https://boostroom.com/blog/heroes-of-the-storm-ranked-guide-how-storm-league-works-and-how-to-climb]
- Tank: dives in alone with no follow up; ignores soak and rotations; uses engage ult with no one in range [judgment].
- Healer: stands in front, spends mana on poke so they have none for the fight, heals the person who is not in danger [judgment].
- Ranged assassin: over extends for one extra auto attack; ignores wave and gets punished by dive [judgment].
- Bruiser or offlaner: stays in a lane too long after level 10 and misses the fight, or leaves a lane open before an objective [judgment; partly from a Reddit opinion on bruiser tempo].
- Support or specialist: Abathur and similar not placing mines or using global actively (forum link above).

## What low to mid rank players misuse
- Picking for damage only; ignoring CC, survivability and utility. [source: Boostroom link above]
- Ignoring wave clear, which loses games without losing fights. Same source.
- Using mercenary camps just because they are up. Same source and Dignitas camp article. [source: https://dignitas.gg/articles/blogs/Heroes-Of-The-Storm/11768/heroes-of-the-storm-how-to-fully-utilize-mercenary-camps]
- Hoarding gems, doubloons or tributes instead of turning in (map guide B).
- Blaming the draft after the game starts (Boostroom).
- Rank advice: Bronze and Silver should stay with a small pool and basics; Gold and Platinum add camp timing and cooldown tracking (Boostroom).
