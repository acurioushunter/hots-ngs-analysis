# Draft theory (written 8 Oct 2026)

Source quality warning: the public pages reachable today are thin. TL.net, Icy Veins and the fandom wiki returned 403 or 402. Most sourced lines come from a Boostroom ranked guide (Aug 2026), an Inven article on the three ban era (old), and GosuGamers pieces from about 2015. Archetype definitions have NO Heroes specific public source found, so they are tagged [judgment] and should be checked against Hunter's own game data. ## Format and information
- Storm League: map known before the draft, three bans per team (two early, one later), each hero once, draft locks at the loading screen. [source: https://boostroom.com/blog/heroes-of-the-storm-ranked-guide-how-storm-league-works-and-how-to-climb]
- A guide author claims draft is worth 40 to 60 percent of the outcome. That is an estimate, not measured. [source: https://tl.net/forum/heroes/525618-bro-do-you-even-draft-a-guide-to-hl-drafting] (seen via search snippet only)

## First pick versus map pick
- The map is known before heroes are picked, so the team with map choice picks the map where its comp and pools are best, and the other side gets first pick or last pick value [judgment].
- Last pick (slot 16 in NGS) sees everything. It is the best slot for a counter or a gap fill, but it only works if the pool is deep enough that a ban cannot strip it [judgment].
- First picks reveal information. Take heroes that are strong on any comp (a flexible tank, a top healer, a hero the other team would otherwise ban late) and keep specialists for later. A glossary level principle, not Heroes specific. [judgment]
- 2015 era pro advice: first pick strong mages and take good wave clear on rotation maps, then counter pick. Dated but the logic is still sound. [source: https://www.gosugamers.net/heroesofthestorm/news/41011-kaeyoh-on-the-current-draft-meta] (snippet only)

## Ban logic
- Early bans go on the hero that decides games on this map or the opponent's best player hero. When bans arrived, pros expected target banning of players with narrow pools: "weak hero pools get punished pretty hard". [source: https://www.invenglobal.com/articles/5654/hgc-pros-speculate-on-the-three-hero-ban-meta-weak-hero-pools-get-punished-pretty-hard]
- Bans also push the draft toward second tier heroes: when top ranged damage is banned, previously counter picked heroes see play. Same source.
- A ban can be spent on the best remaining support or healer, since a team can deny it with a ban and first pick. Same source (Shad's view).
- Late ban answers the board: the hero that completes their comp or counters ours [judgment, matches draft_tips.md].
- Do not ban what a player is bad at. Check the player's record first [judgment].

## Flex picks and denial
- A flex hero can fill two roles (a bruiser who can offlane or tank, a support who can play second healer). It delays commitment so the other side cannot counter pick cleanly. Icy Veins' baseline: 1 tank, 1 offlaner, 1 ranged assassin, 1 flex, 1 healer. [source: https://boostroom.com/blog/heroes-of-the-storm-ranked-guide-how-storm-league-works-and-how-to-climb] (Boostroom states the same baseline.)
- Denial: taking the strongest AoE or the best healer so the opponent cannot. A 2015 article names "Fire and Ice" (take both strongest AoE heroes) as a team fight edge. [source: https://www.gosugamers.net/heroesofthestorm/news/38368-8-hero-synergies-that-dominate-the-current-meta]
- Denial costs a pick slot. It is worth it only when the hero is also good in your comp [judgment].

## Counter picks
- Counters are real but smaller than comp and map fit. Read the reason on the hero page, not just the list. [judgment, echoes draft_tips.md]
- Example reasons that are hero specific and sourced: Mephisto punishes dive and wants spread teamfights; Garrosh punishes heroes who walk into his range early. [source: https://icy-veins.com/heroes/tier-list] (via search snippet, 2026 status unverified)
- Respect the counters that come from kit, not stats: sustained displacement versus a team that needs to line up shots, cleanse versus single target lockdown, blind or armor versus auto attack carries [judgment].

## Healer and tank priority
- A missing healer or front line is the hardest hole to fix late [judgment, in draft_tips.md]. Fragile ranged damage wants protection: Valla "loves" a second healer or a support. [source: https://www.icy-veins.com/heroes/valla] (snippet; not fetched)
- Gap fill list to check before each late pick: front line, wave clear, damage, camp clear, backline protection. [source: https://boostroom.com/blog/heroes-of-the-storm-ranked-guide-how-storm-league-works-and-how-to-climb]

## Comp archetypes (all [judgment] unless noted; no Heroes specific sourced taxonomy was found)
- Dive: tank plus melee assassin plus CC, kills a priority target fast. Beats poke and fragile backlines when it gets on them. Loses to peel heavy sustain, spread positioning and hard CC on the diver, and to wave clear siege that wins without a fight.
- Poke: ranged damage and siege before the fight. Beats slow engage and objective stand offs. Loses to dive, to anything with global or gap closing engage, and to teams that avoid poking by taking the objective early.
- Pick: stuns, hooks and long range lockdown to delete one hero. Beats comps that walk in a line. Loses to teams that group tightly with cleanse and shields.
- Sustain: healer stacking with tanky bruisers. Beats burst light poke and slow attrition. Loses to anti heal, big AoE and comps that win by objective rather than fights.
- Four man push or split: one or two heroes soak or split while the team collects camps or forces rotations. Beats teams that cannot clear waves or defend three lanes. Loses to burst that catches the lone splitter and to global presence. A 2015 article also lists Johanna with Abathur hat as an early map control threat. [source: https://www.gosugamers.net/heroesofthestorm/news/38368-8-hero-synergies-that-dominate-the-current-meta]
- Deathball: five together, big team fight talents and ults. Beats split and poke if it can connect. Loses to a team that keeps waves and objectives while avoiding fights, and to big AoE.
- Wave clear siege: heroes that clear waves fast and siege structures from range. Beats teams that cannot hold objectives and map pressure. Loses to dive, and to a deathball that forces the fight on its terms.
- Archetype choice follows the map: Infernal Shrines wants AoE to clear guardians, Tomb and Cursed Hollow want wave clear and rotations (see map_strategy.md).

## What changes by skill level
- Bronze to Silver: consistency over cleverness. Small hero pool, wave clear, no needless deaths, be at objectives on time. Draft mistakes at this level are mostly missing roles. [source: https://boostroom.com/blog/heroes-of-the-storm-ranked-guide-how-storm-league-works-and-how-to-climb]
- Gold to Platinum: map specific drafting, talent tier awareness, camp timing, cooldown tracking, punishing rotations. Same source.
- Diamond to Master: draft adaptation, counter picks, vision control, precise camp timing, using talent advantages aggressively, map specific win conditions. Same source.
- Pro and NGS level: bans target player pools, draft is about denying the opponent's best two heroes while keeping own pools open, and each player has one or two heroes far above the rest. Cross check with the project's per player data. [judgment, partly supported by the Inven article above]
- Common pitfall at every level: picking only for damage and ignoring CC, survivability and utility. Same Boostroom source.
- Common pitfall: blaming the draft after the game starts. Same Boostroom source.
