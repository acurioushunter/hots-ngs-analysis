## 28 Sep 2026 patch contents
- Xal'atath kit as released: Shadow Mark Q deals 75 plus 150 in the center and marks for 4 seconds; Dark Heart Ritual W pulses 50 per second for 8 seconds and detonates for 305 (plus 150 on the marked target); Void Step E teleports three times and boosts basic attacks 10 percent; trait Void Volley fires after 5 basic attacks, 6 orbs at 90 each. PTR numbers were lower, so use live numbers only. [source: https://jeu.video/article/hots-patch-xalatath] (via search summary)
- Seven hero changes were on the 14 Sep PTR: Abathur, Alexstrasza, Garrosh, Mal'Ganis, Qhira, Whitemane, Yrel. Known numbers: Alexstrasza base health 1698 to 1780; Garrosh Unrivaled Strength bonus 125 percent to 75 percent; Whitemane Desperate Plea mana 40 to 45. Abathur, Mal'Ganis, Qhira, Yrel numbers not found. [source: https://jeu.video/article/heroes-of-the-storm-xal-atath-im-ptr-mit-balance-aenderungen-fuer-sieben-helden]
- The live version may differ from the PTR (Icy Veins marks PTR to live changes). Unverified which of the PTR numbers survived.
- Garden of Terror: Shambler starts with 25 percent less health and damage, then grows back to the old values over the match (time not stated). [source: same jeu.video link above]
- Why it matters for us: Qhira is in our deep dive material, so re-check her numbers from the official post before relying on any Qhira note. Garrosh got a damage nerf, so a Garrosh ban is less urgent than before [judgment].

## 11 May 2026 balance patch (still in force unless changed since)
- Haunted Mines: skull scaling 5 percent to 7 percent; Golem scaling before 5 minutes lowered, later scaling significantly increased (no numbers). [source: https://news.blizzard.com/en-us/article/24276959/heroes-of-the-storm-balance-patch-notes-may-11-2026]
- Arthas: health 2625 to 2750, Death Coil healing 262 to 275, Frostmourne Hungers per stack 1 to 0.75, charged attack bonus 1 to 3, Frost Presence slow benefit 0.75s to 1.25s, Path of Frost minimum movement speed 140 percent. Same source.
- Chen: Flying Kick 120 to 130, Fortifying Brew shield tick 90 to 95, Accumulating Flame requirements 20/75 to 25/80. Same source.
- Deathwing: Molten Flame tick 19 to 20; Destroyer's Rampage duration 6s to 4s. Same source.
- Gazlowe: Rock-it Turret damage 37 to 43; One Man Wrecking Crew bonus 60 to 75 percent. Same source.
- Mal'Ganis: Will of Tichondrius bonus per stack 0.5 to 0.25 and quest requirement 40 to 20; Winged Guard every 6s to 5s; Deep Sleep speed 10 to 15 percent; Wrath of Nathreza transfer 75 to 85 percent. Same source.
- Murky: March of Murlocs cooldown 100s to 90s. Same source.
- Commentary (secondary): frontline and objective control drafts gained value, especially on Haunted Mines; Arthas and Chen better on tight choke maps. [source: https://www.gamer.org/heroes-of-the-storms-may-11-balance-patch-hits-haunted-mines-buffs-tanks-and-trims-deathwing/] Author opinion, not data.

## Other 2026 changes
- 19 Feb: Deathwing Incinerate cooldown 6s to 7s; Muradin Storm Bolt quest 50/200 to 40/160; Death Drop Spell Armor 6s to 4s. [source: https://patchbot.io/games/heroes-of-the-storm]
- 20 Apr: Unranked Mode removed; mid to late respawn timers shortened. Same source. Shorter respawns lower the cost of a lost fight, so objective pushes after a won fight matter slightly less and defending is easier [judgment].
- 20 Jul: Haunted Mines added to Ranked; can party with unplaced players in Storm League. Same source. Warhead Junction interrupted launch penalty 10s to 5s per a map guide (date "July 2026" unverified).
