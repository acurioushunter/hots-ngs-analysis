Method: Archetype = comps.classify() on ngs_player_game heroes using hero_tags.HEROES. Primary tag scores 2 and secondary 1, summed into Poke/siege (poke), Dive/engage (dive, engage), Brawl/sustain (brawl, sustain). The leading group names the comp; if the top two groups are within 1 point it is Balanced. push and burst tags score nothing. Chen, Murky, Probius, Samuro, Sgt. Hammer and Zeratul are untagged but were only banned, never picked.
Sample is tiny, read every number as a lean. LOW means n under 8. Each game counts once per side, so matchup rows are mirrored.
