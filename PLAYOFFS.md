# C West playoff picture (as of Tue Oct 6 2026, before tonight's PRA v COSMOS result is reported)

Sources: nexusgamingseries.org division page (standings and agenda) and the NGS rules page (sections 3 and 6). Results by pair come from our own Season 22 game data.

## Rules that matter
- Each match is best of 3 games. 2-0 gives the winner 4 points. 2-1 gives the winner 3 and the loser 1.
- C West is a double round robin: 10 fixed matches each. The regular season ends Sunday Oct 18, playoffs run Oct 19 to Nov 22.
- **All 6 C West teams make the playoffs** (top 6 of the West coast). Single elimination, every game best of 5. The first playoff week lasts 2 weeks, each later round 1 week. The West winner meets the East winner in the grand final.
- Ties in points: head to head points, then map score (map wins minus map losses), then coin flip.
- The rules do not spell out the 6 team bracket. The usual layout is assumed here: seeds 1 and 2 get a bye, 3 v 6 and 4 v 5 play first, then 1 plays the 4/5 winner and 2 plays the 3/6 winner. Check the Tournament tab on the division page once seeds lock.
