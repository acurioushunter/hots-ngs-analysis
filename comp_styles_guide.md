# HotS comp styles and hero notes (general knowledge, not from the match data)

## Comp styles
**Poke / siege**: win by chipping the enemy down from range (Junkrat, Sylvanas, Kael'thas, Chromie, Hanzo, Jaina, Lunara, Tassadar, Blaze, Mei, Azmodan).
- Strength: free damage, strong on objective timers and in tight lanes, camp and fort pressure.
- Weakness: dies to a hard engage or dive (Johanna, Diablo, Garrosh, Varian, Alarak, Illidan). Needs peel and a way to start or avoid fights.
- Beat it by: diving the backline, trading forts, staying out of range until you can force a full commit.

**Dive / engage**: win by jumping onto squishy targets and trading cooldowns (Garrosh, Diablo, Varian, Thrall, Falstad, Alarak, Illidan, Valeera, Brightwing, Anduin).
- Strength: fast kills, wins skirmishes, punishes lone enemies and bad positioning.
- Weakness: if the first dive fails or the enemy has stuns or peel, it collapses. Poor at long sieges and objectives without kills.
- Beat it by: staying grouped, saving CC and dive counters (Johanna, Anub'arak, Rehgar, Kharazim, Auriel), not giving free picks.

**Brawl / sustain**: win long, tight teamfights with healing, shields and AoE (Leoric, Thrall, Sonya, Muradin, E.T.C., Johanna, Rehgar, Uther, Tychus, Greymane, Valla).
- Strength: stable, forgiving, strong at objectives where fights are forced (Towers, Infernal Shrines, Braxis).
- Weakness: poor against sustained poke on big open maps, and if the frontline is split or hooked. Struggles with wave clear and pushing.
- Beat it by: poke and pick-offs, anti-heal, forcing fights to be spread out, fast wave clear.

**Wave clear / push**: win with lane control (Abathur, Junkrat, Azmodan, Zagara, The Lost Vikings, Nazeebo, Lunara, Sylvanas, Raynor).
- Strength: trade forts and camps while taking objectives. Weakness: weak in direct fights, so the enemy avoids you.

**Balanced**: two or more win conditions. Easy to draft, but can be out-specialised by a team that commits to one plan.

## Key heroes you play, or play against, often
- **Junkrat**: control and poke mage. Holds an area, roots and displaces with Rip Tire and Concussion Mine, good wave and camp clear. Lacks burst when Rip Tire or his Q is on cooldown and is fragile when caught.
- **Thrall**: brawl bruiser. Great teamfight damage, crowd control and sustain, strongest in four-man plate. Struggles with camps and clearing many minions.
- **Sylvanas**: poke, siege and wave clear. Strong on pressure and in front of a frontline that lets her stand still. Weak to dives and any cooldown on Haunting Wave or Black Arrows.
- **Johanna**: engage tank. Strong CC (Blessed Shield, Falling Sword) and sustain. Poor wave clear and she can't escape a good kite.
- **Anub'arak**: engage and pick tank. Excellent against squishy ranged teams, weak when banned or when enemies kite.
- **Kael'thas, Tassadar**: poke and burst. Cosmos builds around them, so ban or dive them.
- **Leoric**: sustain bruiser. Great frontline for long fights, weaker on poke maps.
- **Greymane, Tychus, Valla**: sustained damage. Reward a peeling team and a good engage.
- **Brightwing**: mobile healer for dive comps. **Rehgar**: brawl healer, strong with Thrall and Leoric. **Anduin**: engage healer.

## How to use this
Check the enemy's likely style, then draft the counter: dive into poke, poke into brawl, and sustain or peel into dive. Draft a wave clearer unless the comp can win without one.
