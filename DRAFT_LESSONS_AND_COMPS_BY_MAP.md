What the data says went wrong:

On Tomb, teams with **3 or more ranged heroes are 8-4 and teams with 2 or fewer are 1-5** (Icy Veins also lists poke tools for the objective as a Tomb want). 2. **Slot 14 was the moment.** chelsi took Thrall with Tychus, Tassadar, Gul'dan, Chromie and Orphea all open. Any of those would have given a safe ranged clear instead of a third melee clearer.
3. **Kael'thas was not banned.** Cosmos is 8-2 when it plays Kael'thas (10 games, SilverJackal) and Kael'thas was their top clearer last night (5.4k minion damage a minute). We banned it in games 2 and 3 and won both. In game 1 our bans were Anub'arak, Anduin and Blaze (our own SoulShepherd's hero).
4. **Muradin was open until slot 16.** NorthrnTouch is 4-0 on Muradin in NGS (47.5% in Storm League over his career), and Cosmos is the team that loses to Muradin and Stitches comps. We took Tyrael instead, which is outside his usual pool (rating 56, 6 deaths), and Cosmos took Muradin last.
One game proves nothing, but all four were fixable at the draft.

**A cleaner Tomb draft against Cosmos (first pick):** bans Kael'thas, Anub'arak, then Greymane at slot 11. Picks: Sylvanas or Chromie, then Johanna or Muradin, Brightwing, a ranged clearer for chelsi (Tychus, Tassadar or Gul'dan) at 14 at the latest, and Leoric or Blaze. That is 3 to 4 ranged heroes.

## 2. Was Chromie an option? Yes
- **Open the whole draft in game 1.** Cosmos has **never banned Chromie** (0 of 21 games). CCS has banned it in 3 of 6 games against us (always round one), so against CCS it may be gone.
- **Your form on it:** 12-3 (80%) in Storm League over the last year (9 games since the rework, 7-2), 1-1 in NGS (the win was last night, 59 rating, 5/12/1). chelsi has played it 2-1 this season.
- **Its clear:** 3.0k minion damage a minute in this division, ranged, so it is a safe clearer. Chromie is countered by Illidan, Diablo, Zeratul and Tracer, which none of Cosmos plays.
- **The caution:** across the whole division Chromie is only 4-6 in 10 games, and your NGS loss on it (Aug 18, Dragon Shire against CCS) was a 47 rating with 6 deaths.
- **Verdict:** a good pick into Cosmos, including on Tomb, instead of Sylvanas if the comp is short on clear. Sylvanas is better when structure pressure is what we are missing.

## 4. Your Tychus and Li-Ming idea, tested
**Last night on Battlefield of Eternity (13:30 win)** the numbers say **Blaze did the lane clearing** (5.0k minion damage a minute) while **Li-Ming (3.0k camp damage a minute, 5 camps) and Tychus (2.7k, 4 camps) took the camps.** Their own minion damage was low (1.3k and 1.9k). Johanna also captured 5 camps. Our five took 20 camps to Cosmos's 5, which is what freed you up to poke and take the objective fast. So it worked, but through camps and poke, not through Ming and Tychus clearing waves.

**Are they weak on beefy camps?** In part, and on Tomb:
- **Tychus on Tomb: 484 camp damage a minute and 0.9 camps per 10 minutes over 6 games (3-3),** against 0.9k to 2.9k on the other maps (1.2k on average). That fits your worry. Gul'dan (2.1k), Greymane (1.6k), Junkrat (1.3k) and Sylvanas (0.9k) did more on Tomb. Brightwing (1.8 camps per 10 minutes, 4-0 on Tomb) and Rehgar (1.4) captured the most camps among our regulars there.
- **On Garden of Terror both are fine:** Tychus 1.4k a minute (2 games, 1-1), Li-Ming 1.6k (1 game, win). Tychus 1.2k and Li-Ming 1.2k a minute over all camp heavy maps put them mid pack (Greymane 2.4k, Raynor 2.3k, Gul'dan 2.0k, Falstad 1.6k, Junkrat 1.5k, Sylvanas 1.5k).
- **Garden's real signal is the comp, not those two heroes:** the wins came with two ranged heroes plus a double front and Rehgar. A Tychus and Li-Ming comp has 4 ranged heroes with Blaze and Brightwing, which is the shape that went 1-4 on Garden.
- Samples are 2 to 6 games per hero per map. Treat the Tomb camp number as a flag, not a fact.

## 5. Draft rules from this
1. **Count ranged before the last two picks.** Tomb wants 3 or more. Garden and Braxis lean the other way (two ranged, a double front, Rehgar).
2. **Ban Kael'thas first against Cosmos,** especially on Tomb.
3. **Lock the hero Cosmos fears (Muradin) before slot 16.** NorthrnTouch should not end on Tyrael.
4. **chelsi's slot 14 pick is a ranged clearer when we have two or fewer ranged heroes** (Tychus, Tassadar, Gul'dan, Chromie).
5. **Hunter's choice on Tomb:** Chromie or Orphea when we are short on clear, Sylvanas when we have it. Your Storm League record on Tomb over the last year is 31-39, so do not expect Sylvanas alone to carry it.
6. **On camp maps (Garden, Dragon Shire, Towers, Shrines, Braxis, BoE, Cursed Hollow, Sky Temple) win the camp count:** put Rehgar or Brightwing, Sylvanas and the offlaner on camps deliberately.
