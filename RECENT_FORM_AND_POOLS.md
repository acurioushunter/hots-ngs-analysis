### What Sylvanas gives you
- She is your main and your most reliable hero: 66-46 (58.9%) since the rework, and **41-11 (79%) at 2 deaths or fewer against 25-35 (42%) at 3 or more.**
- **Her value is structure pressure, not minion damage.** Per game last year Sylvanas did **25.4k structure damage** (Junkrat 17.2k, Orphea 15.2k), 3.6 camp captures and 13.9k experience, but only 46k minion damage against Junkrat's 89k. That matches what you described: Possession turns enemy minions into pushers (Icy Veins: it forces an enemy Minion to fight for her team, 4 charges, catapults cost 3), and her Mind Control heroic gives crowd control to follow up kills. In NGS this season your Sylvanas does 1.5k structure damage and 1.6k camp damage a minute, level with the other damage heroes here (Tychus 1.5k and 1.5k, Lunara 1.6k and 1.7k).
- **The catch:** she does not clear waves by herself (2.0k minion damage a minute in this division), so her team needs other clearers on maps that ask for wave clear.

### Your pick order for NGS from this
1. **Sylvanas** first (no CCS or Cosmos pick counters it, and UnicycleYay plays it).
2. **Junkrat** if it is open (it usually is not).
3. **Orphea** next: ranged, 3.7k minion damage a minute (a strong clearer in this division), 70.6% in Storm League over 17 games, 3-0 in NGS Season 21, and CCS has never banned it.
4. **Chromie**: 80% over 15 games, 2.8k minion damage a minute, 1-1 in NGS (the win was last night), counters Gazlowe. CCS bans it in round one about half the time.
5. Cassia (62.5%), Mephisto (63.6%) and Tychus (70%, but it is chelsi's) as flex.
6. **Raynor is a practice pick, not a plan.** It is 6-2 over only 8 games in the last year, so we do not know how it plays in team practice. Icy Veins says it counters Dehaka, Gazlowe and Arthas, but we would be guessing.
Avoid Hanzo, Medivh, Li-Ming and Falstad in Storm League.
