# Qhira and UnicycleYay: why CCS loses with her, and what we do about it

Source: all 18 CCS games in `knowledge/ngs_div.db` plus the raw match pages (fight damage, time dead). Five games have CCS playing Qhira (all UnicycleYay), so every number is a lean.

## The short version
1. **Qhira is not what makes UnicycleYay good. It is his worst hero.** On Qhira he is 2-3 with a 55.6 average rating and 4.0 deaths a game. On everything else he is **8-2**: Sylvanas 3-1, Hanzo 2-1, Tychus 2-0, Valla 1-0, with 1.0 to 1.7 deaths a game.
2. **So banning Qhira can help CCS, not us.** The one game we banned it in round one and he took Sylvanas, he went 7/0/9 (71 rating) and we lost 15 minutes in. His marksmen are the real threat.
3. **The three Qhira losses were all against Cosmos, and all three were lost on deaths and poke, not on the hero.** Qhira itself was weak in them (39k to 58k damage, 47 to 50 rating).
4. **The Garden game he won against us was won by wave clear.** Ragnaros and Li-Ming did the work. Qhira had a good game, but the 12.0k against 18.2k minion damage gap is bigger than any one hero.
5. **The odd damage with Tassadar and Falstad (Sep 22 game 3, Infernal Shrines) was chip damage with no follow up, on a draft where Hunter lost Sylvanas.** Details below.

- **Same build in all five games** (Maximum Effort, Upstage, The Thirst, Unrelenting Strikes, Chainsaw, Booming Kick, Silent Killer). Nothing about the talents explains wins and losses.
- **Deaths decide it again.** In both wins CCS died fewer times than the other team. In all three losses they died more (13 v 6, 18 v 2, 18 v 15), with takedowns 26 against 59 on Tomb and 9 against 77 on Towers.
- **Poke:** in the two worst losses they were outpoked by 1.1k to 2.8k a minute. Our earlier finding applies here too: the poke margin tracks the result in this division.
- **Qhira's own damage collapses in losses:** 1.6k to 3.3k a minute against 3.2k to 3.9k in wins. She took 36k to 62k damage in fights, which makes her the front of their dive, not a safe carry.

## What Cosmos did three times (the pattern that beat her)
| Game | Cosmos draft |
|---|---|
| Tomb | Johanna, Tassadar, Brightwing, Blaze, Tychus |
| Towers | Kael'thas, Anub'arak, Anduin, Greymane, Blaze |
| Shrines | Anub'arak, Brightwing, Tassadar, Valla, Rexxar |
Common shape: **a frontline with a stun or charge (Johanna or Anub'arak), a healer with crowd control (Brightwing or Anduin), one big ability mage (Tassadar or Kael'thas) and a strong clearer (Blaze, Rexxar).** This fits what Icy Veins says about her: every ability except Blood Rage can be interrupted, so heroes that stun and knock back keep her from landing her skillshots. Icy Veins' listed counters are E.T.C., Diablo, Garrosh, Lunara and Tracer. That is the guide's reasoning plus these three games, not something the stats prove by themselves (3 games).
Icy Veins also says she is weak on enclosed maps (Towers, Shrines) and strong on open ones (Dragon Shire, Tomb). CCS lost with her on two of the weak maps, but also on Tomb, a strong one, so the map alone is not the cause. CCS is 4-1 on Towers and 2-1 on Shrines overall, so we still ban both.

## The Garden game she won against us (Sep 22 game 2)
- PRA drafted Rehgar, Sylvanas, E.T.C., Blaze, Tychus. CCS drafted Qhira (first pick, slot 5), Anub'arak, Li-Ming, Ragnaros, Tyrande.
- Qhira: 77.6k damage, 66k minion damage, 27k camp damage, 8 camps. The team numbers that mattered more: **CCS minion damage 18.2k a minute against our 12.0k**, with Ragnaros alone at 265k minion damage and 351k siege damage, and Li-Ming at 120k siege.
- Our side: Tychus did 84k, but Sylvanas only 51.5k, and **E.T.C. died 6 times (rating 47)**, which sent our fights into overtime without him. We lost 15 deaths to 11.
- Read: the loss was lane and clear control plus E.T.C. dying, not Qhira being unstoppable. Our draft had two ranged heroes and no one who could answer Ragnaros's push.

## Sep 22 game 3: Tassadar and Falstad (Infernal Shrines, 15:21, a loss)
- **The draft:** we banned Qhira first (slot 1), Sonya and Hammer, and first picked Johanna at slot 5. **CCS took Sylvanas at slot 6 and Gazlowe at 7**, which left Hunter on Falstad (44 rating, 0 camps, 1/4/3) and chelsi on Tassadar. The Qhira ban bought us nothing: UnicycleYay went to Sylvanas and finished 7/0/9.
- **Tassadar did the most damage in the game (69k, 4.5k a minute) with a 51 rating and 1 kill.** 41k of it (59%) was poke outside team fights, only 28k in fights. Falstad's 37k was half poke and half fights, and he cleared 68k of minions with zero camps.
- **Why it did not convert:** our team did 5.9k a minute in fights against 8.0k, and Johanna took 63k in fights while dealing 25k. Whitemane healed 90k and Arthas soaked. CCS got 17 takedowns to our 5, 10.6k structure damage a minute to our 1.6k, and we died 17 times to their 5.
- **Poke into a Whitemane and Arthas comp does not kill anyone** unless the fight damage follows. Our two top damage heroes were pokers, our frontline was alone, and the healer had no pressure on her.
- **The lesson is the same as the cheat sheet:** lock Sylvanas at slot 5 so UnicycleYay does not get her, and do not first pick Johanna while Sylvanas is open.

## What we should do
1. **Do not spend a round one ban on Qhira.** Her ban sends him to Sylvanas, Hanzo or Tychus, where he is 8-2.
2. **Deny the marksmen instead.** Sylvanas at slot 5 or 6 (ours), a late ban on **Hanzo** (his highest damage hero, 4.1k a minute) if Tychus is already ours or banned. Tychus is chelsi's second hero, so that one stays in our pool. This replaces the old "Hanzo or Sylvanas at slot 11" guess with a reason.
3. **If Qhira is open and he takes her, treat it as a good sign and punish it.** Pick the Cosmos shape: a frontline with a stun (Johanna if she is open, otherwise Varian or Muradin), Brightwing or Rehgar with crowd control, and a ranged damage hero with poke. Keep our fights compact so she cannot flank.
4. **Kill her in fights.** She takes 36k to 62k damage a game in fights, so she is the front of their dive. If we kill her, the 1.6k to 3.3k damage numbers appear.
5. **Pick a clearer that answers Ragnaros and Li-Ming on camp maps** (Garden): Leoric, Blaze, Tassadar. Do not ask Sylvanas to clear.
6. **If E.T.C. is on the table, NorthrnTouch should play it only with a plan.** It is in Icy Veins' counter list, but he was 1/6/7 on it in the Garden game and 2-2 this season.
