## 6. Camping, fighting, wave clear

- The league data says fight well and stay alive. Deaths, takedowns, structure damage and camp damage line up with the winner 88 to 90% of the time. These are outcomes as much as causes, so read them as what winning looks like.
- Wave clear (minion damage) lines up with the winner only 40% of the time. The team that loses often clears more.
- Camps captured line up 75% of the time, but they follow deaths: when we die they camp, when they die we do.
- So "push camps" is not the lever. "Do not give up kills" is. In the Shrines loss our only camp captures came from the healer and Tassadar.

## 7. Plan against CCS (a lean, not a rule)

1. **Take first pick when you have the choice.** Do not choose the map unless it is Sky Temple, Volskaya or Garden.
2. **Map bans.** Towers of Doom (they are 4-1, we are 0-1) and Infernal Shrines (they are 3-1 and 2-0 as the map picker, we are 1-2). They ban Braxis and Cursed Hollow or Alterac. We beat them on Sky Temple and Volskaya and lost to them on Dragon Shire and Alterac, so those two are the maps to watch if they stay in the pool.
3. **Round one bans.** Johanna and Lunara. Not Qhira, not Sonya.
4. **P1 as first pick: Sylvanas** for Hunter, because Junkrat and Chromie will be banned. If they have first pick, take Sylvanas at P2 or P3 along with the healer.
5. **Lock Thrall or Tychus before slot 9** (first pick: by P4 or P5, second pick: by P3).
6. **Third ban:** the strongest hero left among Gazlowe, Lunara, Whitemane, Li-Ming. Never Sgt. Hammer.
7. **Healer:** Brightwing or Anduin. Rehgar only on Garden.
8. **Offlaner and tank:** Blaze and Muradin on Shrines. Leoric is 5-4 for us and 0-4 on Shrines across the division, so keep him off that map.
9. **Hunter's open pool:** Sylvanas, Tychus, Orphea, Raynor, Cassia. Falstad is on the config's avoid list and is 1-3 for us. Raynor (5-2 division wide, 5.6 camps, 30k structures, 1.6 deaths) and Tychus (15-10, 27k structures, 1.7 deaths) fit the safe and siege description best. Gul'dan (5-3, 31k structures) and Kael'thas (8-4, 1.6 deaths) are the next two.
