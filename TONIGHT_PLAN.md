## The draft rules this gives us
- **A pick is also a denial.** No hero has ever appeared on both teams in our 53 games.
- **Johanna is the key hero.** Cosmos is 4-0 on it with Nae, and Icy Veins lists it as a counter to Brightwing, Tychus and Raynor, three of our picks. Take it ourselves (NorthrnTouch plays it well) and Nae is left with E.T.C., Stitches or Muradin, each only 1 or 2 games for him.
- **Leoric is second.** Cosmos is 5-0 on it (oddthought and Icon), and in our hands it beats their Johanna and Blaze, and SoulShepherd plays it.
- **Sylvanas is third, and Hunter's.** Nothing Cosmos plays counters it, but Imbuement plays Sylvanas too (737 Storm League games), so do not leave it too late.
- **Greymane is Imbuement's 5-0 hero.** Brightwing counters it and so does Johanna, and both are on our list.
- **Do not chase Stitches.** Cosmos is weak into Muradin and Stitches comps, but NorthrnTouch is 35% on Stitches. Muradin is the safer way to use that edge, and only if NorthrnTouch is comfortable.

## Bans (3 each)
Anub'arak, Tassadar, and a third on **Kael'thas** (SilverJackal 7-2, their best rated player). If you ban last (slot 10 or 11), use it on what they have shown: Greymane or Ragnaros.

Second choice Tomb of the Spider Queen (PRA 3-0, we beat them there, Hunter Sylvanas 11-6, Raynor 11-4). You are second pick, so they open (they first pick Anduin 4 of 9 games).
- Slots 6 and 7: **Sylvanas and Johanna.**
- Slots 12 and 13: **Brightwing** (Rehgar if you want to stay off Johanna counters) and **Leoric** (Blaze if Leoric is gone).
- Slot 16, last: chelsi reacts. **Tychus** if Johanna is ours or banned, otherwise Chromie.

## If we lose the toss: first pick, they choose the map
Expect Infernal Shrines. Hunter on Shrines: Junkrat 17-15, Sylvanas 6-5, Raynor 5-7, so **Sylvanas, not Raynor.** We are 6-6 as first pick, so stay compact and win early (under 20 minutes our group is 31-12).
- Slot 5: **Johanna.**
- Slots 8 and 9: **Sylvanas and Leoric** (Cosmos has never taken Sylvanas at slots 6 or 7, but has taken Leoric at 7 once).
- Slots 14 and 15: **Brightwing** and Blaze or Leoric. chelsi last.
- Our bans are slots 1, 3 and 11.

## How to play it
- Cosmos is 8-1 with brawl and sustain, 0-2 with poke and siege. Break their healer (Icon) first, and avoid long fights.
- Two frontline, one healer, two damage, at least one push hero (Sylvanas is one). Our no push comps are 1-2.
- Hunter: stay at 2 deaths or fewer. Take towers as well as camps between fights.
- Keep games short. In Storm League your group wins 31-12 under 20 minutes and 3-5 over 25.
